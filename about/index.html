      Better Multiplayer, also known in the community as den2, is the successor to DEN Maps. Originally developed as a fight club arena and tournament-hosting mod, it has since evolved into a full multiplayer overhaul that improves nearly every aspect of Elden Ring’s multiplayer systems.

      The project is maintained by the Duelist Event Network (DEN) team:

      • Axi (axd1x8a) - Lead Developer and Project Lead
      • AmirahTheThe (AmirahSouls) - Original developer of DEN Maps; Co-Developer and Advisor
      • NotKjell - Created most of DEN Maps’ arenas; Advisor and Project Manager
      • ThomasJClark - Co-Developer and Contributor

      Join the Community

      → Join our Discord server – the best place to find games, report bugs, follow development, and connect with the Better Multiplayer community.

      Support Development

      Better Multiplayer is a free, community-driven project. If you’d like to help keep it going, there are two ways to contribute:

      → Patreon – Monthly support with supporter-exclusive perks.

      → Ko‑fi – One-time donations to help cover ongoing costs.

      Every bit of support is genuinely appreciated and goes directly toward development!
