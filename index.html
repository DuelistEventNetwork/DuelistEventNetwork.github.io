      • Better Multiplayer

      An expansion and overhaul of Elden Ring’s co‑operative and competitive multiplayer – built to feel like vanilla multiplayer, but better.

      • Full vanilla multiplayer systems preserved and enhanced: messages, phantoms, hunters, and more
      • Better connection quality with lower latency and faster matchmaking
      • 6-player sessions instead of the vanilla 4-player limit
      • Expanded co-op features including Torrent and Spirit Ashes in multiplayer
      • Enhanced invasion mechanics with improved matchmaking and gameplay
      • Dedicated fight clubs and dueling arenas with QoL improvements
      • No multiplayer restrictions - explore and progress freely with friends
      • Custom team match gamemode with better arenas and respawn mechanics
